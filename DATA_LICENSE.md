# Data and derived-artifact terms

The repository's [MIT license](LICENSE) applies only to the original source
code. It does not relicense medical images, dataset annotations, split
manifests derived from third-party data, or model predictions evaluated on
those data.

## Kermany-FG-derived artifacts

Kermany-FG is derived from Kermany, Zhang, and Goldbaum, *Labeled Optical
Coherence Tomography (OCT) and Chest X-Ray Images for Classification*, Mendeley
Data, Version 2, DOI
[10.17632/rscbjbr9sj.2](https://doi.org/10.17632/rscbjbr9sj.2). The source is
licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Kermany-derived manifest rows and predictions retain that attribution and
license; no source images are redistributed here.

## RSNA-1707-derived artifacts

RSNA-1707 is derived from the
[RSNA Pneumonia Detection Challenge](https://www.rsna.org/education/ai-resources-and-training/ai-image-challenge/rsna-pneumonia-detection-challenge-2018).
The RSNA-derived manifest and predictions remain subject to the challenge's
[terms of use and attribution requirements](https://www.rsna.org/-/media/Files/RSNA/Education/AI-resources-and-training/AI-image-challenge/pneumonia-detection-challenge-terms-of-use-and-attribution.ashx?hash=FF7A635F6DFFAD31A30C8715DFA3B8FC21131543&la=en).
No RSNA or NIH source images are redistributed here. Required dataset and paper
attribution is reproduced in [`data/README.md`](data/README.md).

Users are responsible for obtaining the source datasets independently and for
complying with the providers' current access conditions and terms.
